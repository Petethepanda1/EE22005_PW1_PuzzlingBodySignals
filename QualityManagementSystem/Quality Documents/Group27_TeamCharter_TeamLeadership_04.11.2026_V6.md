# 🛠️ Engineering Team Charter
**1. Team Name**

*PW1 Group 27*

**2. Mission Statement**

*To* *Investigate* *how EMG signals can be* *used to understand human physiology* 

**3. Goals & Objectives**

- Goal 1: To complete the skills matrix, assign responsibilities and agree on clear deadlines with a clear project schedule by the end of Day 1. 

- Goal 2: To test and configure the EMG system and collect three usable recordings of a certain arm movement under certain conditions by the end of Day 3. 

- Goal 3: Develop a MATLAB script that analyses and processes the data recorded to calculate the RMS value and produce data and clear plots by the end of Day 4. 

- Goal 4: To prepare a presentation for Friday with our findings and communicate our overall project results to a non-scientific audience in a clear and direct way.

**5. Resources & Constraints**

- Tools: GitHub for file storage, MATLAB for signal processing, Arduino Nano, MyoWare 2.0 EMG sensor kit 

- Budget: No budget is used, all tools and supplies will be provided by the university 

- Constraints: Kit must be returned by Friday 12:15pm, lab is open only from 9:00-17:00. 

**6. Operating Norms**

- Daily meet up: Arrive at lab 10:00 AM daily until the end of the project week to begin working on our task's separately or together 

- Communication: Any updates or messages that should be informed to all team members are communicated through the MS team's PW1 channel 

- Documentation: Any major changes made should be documented on the MS teams General Channel

- Meeting etiquette: Arrive on time to the lab and notify all team members in advance if anyone is unable to attend with valid reasoning

**7. Decision-Making Process**

- Technical decisions: Will be made based solely on data gathered with technical reasoning available to validate and back up decision 

- Resource Decisions: All team members must agree on major resources being used before making any final decisions

- Emergency fixes: Consult lab technician if any hazardous emergency occurs 

**8. Conflict Resolution**

- Step 1: Direct discussion between involved parties

- Step 2: Use data and testing results to resolve disagreements if possible

- Step 3: Discuss and vote as a team for suggested solutions for the issue or disagreement  

- Step 4: Escalation to lecturers or skill owners if issue remains unresolved 

**9. Team Values & Culture**

- Controversy with civility: Challenge ideas in a respectful manner, and use evidence to resolve disagreement 

- Safety: Abide by lab guidelines, and follow procedure of EMG as instructed

- Privacy: Obtain consent from team member before gathering any personal data 

- Respectful Collaboration: Listen to all team members ideas and be open to discuss changes to improve our final output of the project

- Scientific Honesty: Report results accurately, and include any error, noise, and limitations
